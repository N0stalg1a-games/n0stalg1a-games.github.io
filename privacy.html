ROADZTER: TO THE MOON - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

ROADZTER: TO THE MOON ("the game") does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal information.

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind is included.
  • The game makes no network requests while you play.
  • Your progress (scores, coins, unlocks, and settings) is saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to request, correct, or delete on our side.
