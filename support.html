How to play

  • Tap anywhere to fire a thruster burst and hop upward. Gravity does the rest.
  • Fly through the gaps between the neon towers. Each tower you clear scores a point.
  • Ram the surveillance cams before they scan you. A missed cam costs a shield or a life. Grab meme coins along the way.
  • When the giant mech shows up, stay in the safe corridor between its missile streams. Grab the booster it drops to blow it up.
  • Reach 500 to launch to the moon.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved? On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Are there ads? No. ROADZTER has no ads.

Does the game need an internet connection? No. It plays fully offline.
